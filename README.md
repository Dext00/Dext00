## 👋 About Me

Cloud Native enthusiast with a strong focus on **Docker, Kubernetes, and Infrastructure as Code**.  
I enjoy building, running, and optimizing modern containerized platforms — both professionally and in my homelab.

I’m driven by curiosity, continuous learning, and the desire to turn complex infrastructure into reliable, automated systems. Clean configs are great, but I also thrive in a bit of *“organized chaos.”* 😉

---

## 🚀 What I Do

### 💼 Professional Work

- Administering a WebGIS application on **Kubernetes** in the [STACKIT Cloud](https://stackit.com/)
- Building and maintaining infrastructure using **Terraform (IaC)**
- Working with:
  - PostgreSQL databases
  - S3-compatible object storage
  - OpenSearch
  - Observability (LGTM-Stack)
- Focus on reliability, scalability, and reproducible infrastructure

---

### 🏠 Homelab & Side Projects

My playground for testing, breaking, and improving things. There is always something to do or to improve 🚧:

- Unraid NAS setup and self-hosted services
- Virtualization cluster hosting:
  - [Talos Kubernetes](https://www.talos.dev/) clusters provisioned via Terraform
  - Dedicated VM for Home Assistant
  - OpnSense Firewall
- Self-hosted apps for:
  - Media streaming (Plex)
  - Document management (Paperless-ngx)
  - Password management (Vaultwarden)
  - Gitlab CE
  - LGTM-Stack, Uptime Kuma
- Constantly expanding and refining setups 🔧
